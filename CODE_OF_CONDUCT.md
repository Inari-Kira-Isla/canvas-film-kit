# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions — to be a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information (a real name, address, or contact info) without explicit
  permission — including, ironically enough, inside example content contributed to this repo; see
  `scripts/release-scan.mjs` for the mechanical side of that rule.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers via a GitHub issue (for
non-sensitive matters) or via the private reporting channel described in `SECURITY.md` (for anything
you'd rather not post publicly). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban
any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-specific policy rather than a verbatim adoption of a longer external
template — it covers the same ground (respect, no harassment, a way to report problems) without
importing text this project hasn't actually committed to enforcing at that scale.
