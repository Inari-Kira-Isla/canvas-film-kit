# music demo — "Tide Lines" — pre-production (P0)

> Scaffolded 2026-09-28 by `kit new --profile music` (driver=music, facts=label, rubric=music).
> This is one of canvas-film-kit's own shipped demos (K2 batch) — it exists to prove the kit's
> music profile end-to-end (beat detection -> beat-driven timeline -> gate -> export) on a
> synthetic, CC0, deterministically-generated track with a known ground-truth beat grid.

## Logline

A hand-drawn coastline swells on every beat of a 120bpm synthetic click/hat track
(`audio/track.wav`, generated by `kit make-demo-track` — ground truth in `audio/track.truth.json`),
with a two-line Lorem-style caption whose words light up on the same beat grid. Demonstrates the
kit's `beats.mjs` detector, the beat-hit-rate gate, and the generalised caption painter
(`src/components/captions.ts`).

## Notes

- Duration 20s @ 30fps, 1920x1080, matching `audio/track.wav`'s length.
- `audio/beats.json` was produced by `kit beats audio/track.wav audio/beats.json` (detected
  bpm=120, offset≈0.5003s vs. ground truth bpm=120/offset=0.5s — see `audio/track.truth.json`).
- Lyrics are deliberately neutral/Lorem-style (art, not fact) — the music profile's facts gate only
  requires a `// fact: LABEL.x` tag for a `LABEL.*` constant, and this demo declares none.
- Single scene (`src/scenes/tide-lines.ts`) on purpose, same reasoning as the abstract demo.
